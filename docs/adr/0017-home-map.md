# 17. Home as a map: one stage, one frame, one choreographer

## Context
Design `Home Archipelago` draws the «تعلّم» tab as a sea that scrolls, one island per unit and the current unit's lessons as stones on a trail. A day header (greeting, goal ring, notices) folds away as the map scrolls and comes back on the first scroll up, and the level's header sticks under the top bar. The design is drawn at 360 wide, right to left, and its returns from a lesson are long timelines (the stone fills, the guide hops, the trail draws, a unit's island shrinks and the next one grows) that move pieces belonging to the header, the map and the bubble at once. «تابع» opens the lesson by growing the bubble into the screen, while CLAUDE.md fixes page transitions in the theme.

## Decision
- **One stage over one scroll view.** The map is a `CustomScrollView` whose first sliver is the header's room (`HEAD + 40`). The day header and the level header aren't slivers: they float over the map in `HomeStage`, moved by `StageController`, which folds the header one to one with the scroll, brings it back on the first scroll up, never folds more than the map has scrolled, and snaps it open or shut when the finger stops. Home's own scrolls (back to the lesson, a page search, a celebration's camera) go through the same controller, so the header and the map never disagree. The header is measured: the design's height is its minimum, and a taller one (large text, a long name) moves the map by the difference.
- **One frame, drawn right to left.** All geometry (`geometry/`) is computed in the design's right-to-left space, the x of the 360 board. In English the whole map is mirrored: widgets through `PositionedDirectional`, painters by flipping the canvas. On other widths `MapFrame` keeps a 480 frame at most, centred, scales the x of the stones and the island's art by `frame / 360`, keeps what the design ties to an edge at its distance from that edge, and never scales sizes, fonts, strokes or anything vertical.
- **Motion writes overrides, widgets read them.** `HomeChoreographer` plays the opening, a lesson done, a unit done, the level done and the smaller moments as timelines that only write `MapOverrides` and `HeaderOverrides` (what to draw instead of the data: the stones done so far, a trail segment's share, a bubble's place, a ring's fraction). The map, the header and the bubble draw the data unless an override says otherwise; under reduce motion a timeline jumps to its last frame. A moving piece keeps the same wrappers at rest (opacity 1, no offset, scale 1), so the end of a motion never builds what it moved again (a bubble entering twice, a press lost). The cubit says what changed (`HomeRules.diff` gives a `HomeMoment`); the page plays it on the map as it was drawn the day before.
- **«تابع» grows over home, then the theme's transition runs.** `ContainerReveal` is an overlay on home that grows from the bubble to the screen; the route is pushed under it with the theme's own transition, and the overlay fades once that transition has had its time. Until the push it takes every tap, so one «تابع» opens one lesson and nothing under it is left meanwhile; if home is gone by then, it clears without pushing. Under reduce motion it is a plain push.

## Consequences
The painters cache a picture per island state, so a timeline that changes one value per frame records one picture per distinct value. A new motion is a timeline over the overrides, with no change to the widgets that draw. The overlay before the push isn't a per-route transition, but it is only checked in tests so far (STATUS D-099). Moving the tabs to a `StatefulShellRoute` later keeps this page as it is.
